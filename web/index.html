Welcome to Dancing Earth

Radar on the NISAR satellite sees Earth's surface through cloud, smoke and darkness. Here you can watch the surface change, and hear it.

  1. Pick a topic at the top: Nature (floods, fires, hurricanes) or Human activity (forest clearing, farming), then a place.
  2. Press Play to step through every satellite pass and hear the change as music.
  3. Try Optical to see what a camera saw the same day, often just cloud, and drag the slider.
  4. Open Live world for today's events, satellites overhead, and a radar image of any place on land.

Follow a satellite

Everything on the flat map works here too: pick a topic at the top, use the layers, the timeline and the tools, and tap the radar image to chart a spot. Tap far away from the current place to load the newest NISAR image there. Drag turns the Earth (or circles the satellite you follow), scroll or pinch zooms down to the ground, Ctrl + drag or two fingers tilt the view. Earth, orbits, heights and the radar beam are to scale; the satellite models are simplified and enlarged about 4,000 times (the ISS about 450 times). Imagery: NASA Blue Marble and Landsat mosaic. Orbits: CelesTrak.
